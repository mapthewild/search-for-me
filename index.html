The fine print

What if I don’t know anyone at these companies?

Then I look for someone who could introduce you. If there’s no one, I say so, and those companies move down the list.

How is this different from asking an AI chat?

A chat starts from what you type. I start from the people you already know, and look for jobs on the companies’ own sites. Say a company posts a job you’d be good at, and you know no one there, but the person who posted it used to work with an old colleague of yours. I find that link, say how sure I am of it, and draft a short note to them: you saw the job, and here’s why you’re interested. You send it when you want to.

Do you apply for jobs for me?

No. You do the talking. I find where to look and who to ask, and I draft your first messages. I never contact anyone for you.

What happens to my LinkedIn data?

You don’t need it to start. If you want the part about who you already know, I ask for it after our first call. Your contacts file stays on my computer. Claude, the AI I use, reads it to match your contacts to companies. I delete your files 60 days after our last session.

How much of my time does it take?

One call to start, where we talk. You don’t need a CV or any files for it. Then a page to read at the end of each week. The idea is to surface people you should be connecting with early enough, so that when you’re ready, that’s already lined up for you.
